🚨 I'm Being Harassed

Focus on safety first. You do not need to argue with or convince the person harassing you.

If there is a direct threat:
Do not meet the person alone. Move somewhere safe, tell a trusted person, preserve the threat, and seek appropriate local help.
1. Pause & don't engage.
Avoid retaliating or sending threats back. This can escalate the situation.
2. Preserve evidence.
Save screenshots, usernames, profile links, dates/times and relevant messages.
3. Restrict access.
Use block, mute, restrict or privacy controls where appropriate.
4. Report.
Report the account/content through the platform's safety or reporting options.
5. Tell someone.
Choose a trusted friend, family member, teacher, university authority or appropriate support service.

📸 Save the Evidence

Evidence can help you explain what happened to a platform, trusted person, university authority or appropriate service.

Best practice: preserve the original context when possible. Keep a private backup somewhere secure.
☐ Screenshot the message, post, comment or profile.
☐ Record the username/profile name and profile link if available.
☐ Note the date and approximate time.
☐ Keep threatening messages or repeated-contact patterns together.
☐ Save relevant emails or notification messages.
Don't: edit screenshots to make them look more dramatic, publicly repost harmful content, or forward intimate material.

📱 Report & Block

Apps change their buttons from time to time. Usually, open the post, message or profile menu → choose Report → select the closest reason → submit. Then Block or Restrict if you need to.

Facebook
Open the profile/post/comment menu → find the report option → choose the closest safety category → submit. Then use block/restrict controls if needed.
Instagram
Open the profile/post/message options → report the relevant content/account → choose the closest category. Consider restrict/block and privacy controls.
WhatsApp
Open the chat/contact information → use the reporting option when available → block the account if needed. Preserve important messages before removing the chat.
TikTok
Open the relevant content/profile menu → report → choose the closest category. Use block and privacy controls where appropriate.
YouTube
Use the content menu/report option → choose the relevant reason. For repeated harassment, also review account/channel blocking and comment controls.
Tip: If the abuse is serious, save evidence before blocking or deleting anything.

🔐 Account Safety

Complete these basics regularly.

Use a long, unique password for important accounts.
Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) where available.
Review logged-in devices and sign out of unknown sessions.
Check recovery email/phone information.
Review profile visibility, tagging and message settings.
Never share passwords or one-time login codes.

🔒 Private Content Protection

Think about where personal information can travel once it is shared.

Before sharing: ask whether the image/video reveals your face, location, school, room, documents or other identifying details.
Review access: check who can view, download, comment on or reshare your content.
Never send intimate content under pressure. Pressure, threats or blackmail are reasons to seek support—not to comply.
If private content is shared without consent: preserve evidence, report it, block/restrict the account where appropriate, and tell a trusted person.

💬 Unwanted Messages

You are allowed to set boundaries online.

Low-risk situation: you may send one clear boundary such as “Please stop contacting me.” Then stop responding.
Repeated contact: mute/restrict/block and preserve evidence.
Threatening or sexual messages: don't negotiate or send more content. Save evidence and involve a trusted person.
Unknown accounts: avoid opening suspicious links or sharing personal information.

👥 Bystander Guide

If someone you know is being targeted, your support can reduce isolation without increasing the risk.

Listen privately.
Believe them enough to take their concern seriously; don't demand proof in public.
Don't forward the harmful content.
Sharing it can increase the harm and spread private material.
Help preserve evidence.
Offer to help organize screenshots or links securely.
Report harmful content.
If safe and appropriate, use the platform's reporting tools.
Stay with them.
Encourage support from a trusted adult, university authority or appropriate service when the situation is serious.

🌱 After the Incident

Online abuse can leave you stressed, embarrassed or unsure what to do next. Recovery is also part of digital safety.

1
Step away.
Take a break from the account or platform if you need one.
2
Keep support around you.
Talk to a trusted friend, family member, teacher or university authority.
3
Secure your account.
Change passwords, review devices and update privacy settings.
4
Follow up.
Keep report numbers, screenshots or relevant links in one secure place.
Remember: Being targeted online is not your fault. You do not have to handle everything alone.

📣 Build a Safer Community

CyberZen is not only about individual protection. Safer online spaces also depend on what bystanders, friends and communities choose to do.

🚫 Report, Don't Revenge
Use reporting tools instead of retaliation.
🤐 Don't Forward Abuse
Do not spread screenshots, private photos or humiliating content.
🤝 Support, Don't Shame

🧠 Think Before You Share
Ask whether your post could expose, embarrass or endanger another person.

⚖️ LEGAL SUPPORT

Know Your Rights. Know Where to Get Help.

Online harassment can have real-world consequences. This section gives general information about legal support in Bangladesh and shows where to seek official help.

🇧🇩 Current Cyber Law

The current government law is the Cyber Security Act 2026. The official Bangladesh Laws website records that it repealed the Cyber Security Ordinance 2025 and replaced the earlier Cyber Security Act 2023.

View Official Law ↗

🛡️ When You Face Online Harassment

  • Save screenshots, URLs, usernames, dates and relevant messages.
  • Keep original evidence safely; do not edit the original files.
  • Block/restrict and report the account when appropriate.
  • For threats, blackmail, stalking, impersonation, doxxing or non-consensual intimate-content sharing, consider making an official complaint.
  • Do not retaliate, threaten the harasser, or redistribute harmful/private content.

👮 Police Cyber Support for Women (PCSW)

Bangladesh Police's PCSW provides legal and technological assistance to women facing cybercrime and can connect victims with an appropriate police unit or victim-support service.

🚨 Immediate Physical Danger?

If an online threat creates an immediate risk to your physical safety, move to a safe place and contact someone you trust. Bangladesh's emergency service is 999.

🆘 Threat & Safety Guide

Threats, blackmail, stalking, impersonation, doxxing or non-consensual intimate-content sharing can be serious. Prioritize physical safety and get help from a trusted person.
Threat of physical harm: move to a safe place and seek immediate local assistance.
Blackmail: don't pay or send additional material just because someone threatens you. Preserve the threats and seek support.
Impersonation: preserve the fake profile/account details and report it to the platform.
Personal information exposed: review account security, remove exposed information where possible, and tell someone you trust.
Intimate content shared without consent: don't redistribute it. Preserve relevant evidence and report the content/account.
